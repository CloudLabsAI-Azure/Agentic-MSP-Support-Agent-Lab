# Seed data specification

The Service Desk data is generated by `assets/labfiles/seed/generate_seed.py` and loaded by `load_seed.py` into each team's Dataverse environment. Generation is deterministic: every team, and every re-run, gets the same records, numbers and answers; only the dates move, by whole weeks, so that the SLA snapshot falls in the current week. `tests/test_seed_determinism.py` checks the counts and the exercise records below for anchors across a year (`python -m pytest setup/tests`).

## Volumes

| Table | Seeded | Minimum the lab needs | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customers (`account`) | 24 | 20 | Enough variety in SLA tier, contract type and product versions for the ambiguous cases |
| Customer procedures (`spk_customerprocedure`) | 63 | 2 per customer | Every customer has escalation contacts and a change rule; some have special procedures the agent must follow |
| Contacts (`contact`) | 72 | 3 per customer | Column security in Exercise 6 needs several contacts per customer |
| Knowledge articles (`spk_knowledgearticle`) | 50 (49 Published, 1 Retired) | 40, with at least 3 conflicting pairs | Grounding, the Retired article, and articles that split on version, tier or contract |
| Runbooks (`spk_runbook`) | 12 | 10 | Procedures for backup, remote access, certificates, major incidents and security |
| Technicians (`spk_technician`) | 12 (1 unavailable) | 10 across 7 teams | Assignment by team and skill, and an unavailable technician the tools refuse |
| SLA definitions (`spk_slapolicy`) | 16 | 4 tiers by 4 priorities | SLA targets for every tier and priority |
| Business calendar (`spk_businesscalendar`) | 1 | 1 | Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC; all SLA figures are in these business hours |
| Tickets (`spk_ticket`) | 474, plus 2 new incidents | 400 history, 40 open | Similar-incident matching, customer histories and SLA risk |
| Ticket notes (`spk_ticketnote`) | 1,203 | 2 per ticket on average | Customer-visible replies and internal notes, including sensitive ones |
| Documents (`annotation`) | 1 | 1 | The sensitivity-labelled customer configuration document |
| Search documents (export) | 561 | | 50 articles, 12 runbooks, 474 tickets, 24 customer profiles and 1 SLA snapshot |

## Values

- Test card numbers are the payment industry's published test numbers; they belong to no account.
- The configuration document uses documentation IP ranges and invented device names.
- Customer, contact and technician names are fictional (Microsoft sample company names and invented people).
